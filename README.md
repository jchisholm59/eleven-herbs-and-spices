# Eleven Herbs & Spices (Air Fryer Edition)

Inspired by a certain Kentucky colonel. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
leaked from anyone. Just a home cook's educated guess.

## The Blend

Whisk into **2 cups all-purpose flour**:

| # | Ingredient        | Amount  |
|---|-------------------|---------|
| 1 | Salt              | 2 tsp   |
| 2 | Paprika           | 4 tsp   |
| 3 | White pepper      | 1 Tbsp  |
| 4 | Garlic salt       | 2 tsp   |
| 5 | Black pepper      | 1 tsp   |
| 6 | Celery salt       | 1 tsp   |
| 7 | Dried mustard     | 1 tsp   |
| 8 | Ground ginger     | 1 tsp   |
| 9 | Dried thyme       | ½ tsp   |
| 10| Dried basil       | ½ tsp   |
| 11| Dried oregano     | ⅓ tsp   |

Optional unofficial 12th: 1 tsp MSG (Accent). Purists say it's the real secret.

## The Chicken

- 8 pieces bone-in chicken (thighs and drumsticks are most forgiving)
- 2 cups buttermilk + 1 egg + 1 tsp salt (the brine)
- Oil spray (peanut or canola)

## Method

1. **Brine:** Soak chicken in the buttermilk/egg/salt mix, refrigerated,
   4 hours to overnight.
2. **Dredge:** Lift each piece, let excess drip, press firmly into the
   seasoned flour. For extra crags, drizzle 2 Tbsp of brine into the flour
   and rub it in with your fingers first.
3. **Rest:** Let coated pieces sit on a rack 10 minutes so the coating
   hydrates and sticks. Don't skip this.
4. **Spray:** Coat every floury spot with oil spray. Dry flour = chalky
   patches.
5. **Air fry:** 380°F (193°C), single layer, 12 minutes. Flip, spray any
   dry spots, 10–14 minutes more.
6. **Check:** 165°F (74°C) minimum; thighs/drumsticks are better at
   175–185°F.
7. **Rest** 5 minutes. Accept compliments.
